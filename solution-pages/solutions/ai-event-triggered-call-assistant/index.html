01

Overview

This project describes a system that places or receives calls in response to business events, then uses an AI voice assistant to understand the caller, provide approved information, and record the outcome. It replaces repetitive manual call handling with a controlled workflow while keeping people involved for exceptions, sensitive requests, and escalation.

A travel company selling India tours and international packages was taking leads from Meta ads, WhatsApp, a website voice agent, spreadsheets, and phone calls, then working them in separate places. We built Akshaya Leads CRM, one lead desk where every enquiry lands in a single pipeline, is tagged as inbound or outbound, is handed to the right staff member, and is followed up until it is booked, lost, or set aside. Claude scores each new lead, and managers can see who is actually working the pipeline.

WhatsApp is one of the lead sources. When a traveller starts the voice agent on WhatsApp, that conversation is saved as a WhatsApp lead, with its own source label, separate from a call that started on the website.

07

Project Impact

The Difference Beyond a Live Conversation

These are operating outcomes the system is built to produce. They are not estimated percentage claims.

  • One queue for every channel. Meta, WhatsApp, website voice, files, and manual entry land on the same pipeline.

  • WhatsApp volume is countable. The dashboard source mix and the team-results source view show WhatsApp on its own.

  • Faster first sorting. New leads are tagged inbound or outbound and given an owner without a manager assigning every row by hand.

  • Fewer missed follow-ups. Due and overdue work is listed separately from the full lead table, in India time.

  • Less repeated writing. The first qualification note and the first WhatsApp or email draft are prepared for review.

  • Clearer team management. Admins can see leads, new leads, bookings, actions, and overdue follow-ups by person, department, and source, then open the actual leads.

  • A path off the spreadsheet. Daily work can run in PostgreSQL while Google Sheets remains available for people who still report from it.
